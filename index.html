About the League

This is REDSEC LEAGUE.

01
For players who are done with random lobbies

You queue, you compete, nothing carries over. RedSec League changes that. Every Drop is tracked. Every kill counts toward your standing. Every week you show up against the same 24 squads who are just as serious as you are.

02
A real league. Not a Discord tournament

25 squads. Tracked standings. A fixed weekly schedule that doesn't move. Accountable leadership that keeps it running. This is organized competition built to last — not a one-off event that disappears after the weekend.

03
The community builds itself

By Drop 4 you will know who your rivals are. By the Final Drop you will have a history with them. That is what RedSec League is building — not just a season, but a competitive community with memory, identity and stakes that matter.
