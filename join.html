Season 1 — Now Recruiting

Join the
League

RedSec League is competitive Battlefield REDSEC. 25 squads. 8 Drops. One lobby. No chaos — just competition. Here is everything you need to know before you register.

01
Read the Rules

Before you register, make sure you and your squad have read the full RedSec League rulebook. Every player is expected to know the rules from Drop 1. Ignorance of the rules is not grounds for a dispute.

Key points — 4 players per squad, 15-minute check-in before every Drop, scores submitted within 1 hour with screenshot evidence. No teaming. No cheating. Command decisions are final.
View Full Rulebook →
02
Form Your Squad

RedSec League requires exactly 4 players per squad. You can register as a pre-formed squad of 4 or as individual players looking to fill slots. If you are looking for players or need a squad, head to the #looking-for-squad channel on our Discord server first.

You may designate 1 optional substitute player at registration. Squad changes are only permitted during the first 2 weeks of the season.
03
Choose Your Franchise

RedSec League has 25 named franchise slots. When you register you will select your preferred franchise from the available list. Franchises are assigned first come first served — once a slot is claimed it is locked for the season.

You will select a first and second choice franchise on the registration form in case your first choice has already been claimed.
View All Franchises →
04
Submit Your Registration

Complete the registration form with your squad details. You will need the in-game name and platform for all 4 players, your squad captain's Discord handle, and your franchise preferences. The form takes less than 5 minutes to complete.

All players must be registered using their actual in-game name. Registrations with incorrect names may be rejected.
05
Join the Discord

Once your registration is confirmed by Command you will receive your franchise role on the RedSec League Discord server. This is where everything happens — Drop night coordination, live scores, standings updates, announcements and the full league community.

Make sure to join the Discord before your first Drop. Check-ins happen in the #lobby-comms channel on Drop night.
06
Compete

Show up every week. Check in on time. Drop hard. Submit your scores. That is the commitment. RedSec League runs on a fixed weekly schedule — every Drop matters and every kill counts toward your standing. Miss a Drop and you receive zero points for that week. There are no make-ups.
